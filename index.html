Lesson Details

  • Class: VIII
  • Subject: Social Studies (History)
  • Topic: How, when And where (Chapter 1)
  • Sub-Topic: Periodisation and colonial Rule
  • Duration: 15 minutes

General Objectives

  • To develop thinking and reasoning power of the Students.
  • To develop the interest of the students in the subject.
  • To enable the students to understand the concept of periodisation.

Learning Objectives

This section breaks down the specific learning outcomes for students, categorized by Bloom's Taxonomy. Click each button to see the specific objectives for that cognitive level.

Remembering

  • Students will be able to remember the definition of periodisation.
  • Students will be able to recall sources of information.

Understanding

  • Students will be able to summarise the concept of colonisation.
  • Students will be able to explain the process of periodisation.

Applying

  • Students will be able to relate the events that are happening in the present day society to that during the colonial period.
  • Students will be able to sketch out the situation Indians were in, during the colonial period.

Analysis

  • Students will be able to analyse the James Mill's periodisation.
  • Students will be able to break down the process of periodisation.

Evaluate

  • Students will be able to evaluate the effects of colonial rule in India.
  • Students will be able to understand the reasoning method of the British people.

Create

  • Students will be able to comprehend to the concept of periodisation.
  • Students will be able to create a short paragraph on the effect of colonial rule on Modern India.

Main Activity Sequence

Step 1

Content: How Do We Periodise

Teacher Activity

The pupil Teacher will explain about the importance of Periodisation. After that the pupil Teacher will explain about James Mill's Periodisation.

Student Activity

The students will listen carefully and make notes when required.

Blackboard Work
  • James Mill's Periodisation:
    → Hindu Era
    → Muslim Era
    → British Era
  • Periodisation (Followed):
    → Ancient India
    → Medieval India
    → Modern India
Step 2

Content: What is Colonial

Teacher Activity

Pupil Teacher will explain about what colonial is and write the important things on the board.

Student Activity

Students will listen carefully and make notes when required.

Blackboard Work

Assessment & Review

This final section outlines the assessment strategy, including checking prior knowledge, reviewing the lesson's key concepts (recapitulation), and assigning follow-up work.

Warm-up (Previous Knowledge Testing)

Pupil Teacher will show a video and ask questions through that video.

  • Q. How long was the mughal period?

    → Expected Response: 350 years

  • Q. How long did the Vedic Age last?

    → Expected Response: 1000 years

  • Q. when did colonisation Begin?

    → Expected Response: 1757

Review (Recapitulation)

  • Q. what do you mean by periodisation?

    → Ans: The process of dividing history into parts to make it easier to study.

  • Q. How did James Mill periodise Indian history?

    → Ans: Hindu Era → Muslim Era → British Era

  • Q. what do you mean by colonial?

    → Ans: Subjugation of one country by another.

Homework

Write your personal views on James Mill's periodisation of Indian history.
